Privacy Policy
Mewsaic Nook · Effective November 3, 2026

THE GAME
Mewsaic Nook works offline. We do not collect or transmit personal data from the game. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, in-app purchases, or social features.

DATA ON YOUR DEVICE
The game saves level progress, collected artwork, current attempts, daily challenge deadlines and results, tutorial status, and sound and haptic preferences on your device. These records support gameplay and are not sent to us. Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to your device settings. We do not operate a cloud-sync service.

YOUR CHOICES
You can change sound and haptic preferences in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app may retain it. A device backup may restore earlier data. Manage backups through your device settings. We cannot recover progress that you have removed.

SUPPORT EMAIL
If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this only to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver this service. Do not send sensitive personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to applicable retention obligations.

CHILDREN
The game does not request personal information from players of any age. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us to request its removal.
