Privacy Policy
Mewsaic Nook · Effective November 7, 2026

THE GAME
Core puzzles work offline and progress stays on your device. No account or in-app purchase is required. The app includes the InMobi advertising SDK. Ad requests can run during startup, even before an ad is displayed. Advertising requires a network connection.

ADVERTISING AND PRIVACY
InMobi and its advertising partners may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, identifiers, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and secure advertising. Your advertising identifier is available only if you allow tracking through the iOS permission prompt. Denying tracking does not prevent gameplay. You can change your choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. ATT permission does not replace regional privacy consent. We do not provide your puzzle progress, contact details, or precise GPS location to the advertising SDK.

DATA ON YOUR DEVICE
The game saves level progress, collected artwork, current attempts, daily challenge deadlines and results, tutorial status, and sound and haptic preferences on your device. These records support gameplay and are not sent to us. Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to your device settings. We do not operate a cloud-sync service.

YOUR CHOICES
You can change sound and haptic preferences in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app may retain it. A device backup may restore earlier data. Manage backups through your device settings. We cannot recover progress that you have removed.

SUPPORT EMAIL
If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this only to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as long as needed for support or legal obligations. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver this service. Do not send sensitive personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to applicable retention obligations.

CHILDREN
The game does not ask players to enter personal information. Advertising may process technical data as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and assist with removal.
